Virtual Career Fair & Networking Hub

A platform specifically for universities to host online career days, where students can join "Booths" and have timed chats with recruiters.
• Key Features: Digital resume drops, "Booth" queue management, and recruiter dashboards for rating candidates.
Detailed Project Overview: This is a specialized virtual ecosystem designed for universities to bridge the gap between students and the professional world. Unlike a standard job board, it simulates a live physical career fair. It allows multiple companies to set up virtual "Booths," where students can engage in timed, private interactions with recruiters. The platform emphasizes real-time logistics, automated queue management, and post-fair data analytics to maximize the employment outcomes for the university's graduating class.
Core Modules
1. Student Portfolio & Engagement Module: Manages student profiles, dynamic resumes, and fair attendance.
2. Recruiter & Booth Operations Module: Handles booth customization, representative scheduling, and candidate evaluation.
3. Real-Time Fair Logistics Module: The engine managing live queues, timed chat sessions, and "Digital Drops."
4. University Admin & Analytics Module: Provides oversight, fair scheduling, and institutional reporting.

A. Student Identity & Portfolio Management (7 Functions)
1. Dynamic Resume Versioning: Logic to manage multiple versions of a resume, allowing students to "attach" a specific version to different company booths.
2. Privacy-Controlled Profile Visibility: An O0 approach to masking sensitive contact data until a recruiter explicitly requests "Full Access."
3. Skill-Gap Analysis Engine: Logic that compares a student's profile against a company's posted job requirements and suggests missing skills.
5. Portfolio Media Handler: Managing the secure upload and viewing of diverse project files (e.g., GitHub links, PDF portfolios, Video intros).
6. Interest-Based Fair Navigator: Logic that highlights specific booths to a student based on their major and pre-set career interests.
7. Student "Ready-State" Controller: Managing the student's status (Available, In-Chat, Offline) to prevent multiple recruiters from initiating contact.
B. Booth Logistics & Queue Management (7 Functions)
8. Automated "Waitroom" Queue Logic: A state-machine that manages the line for a booth, providing students with an "Estimated Wait Time" based on average chat duration.
9. Priority Queueing (Fast-Pass): Logic that allows recruiters to "invite" high-potential students to the front of the queue based on pre-fair resume screening.
10. Multi-Representative Load Balancing: Automatically distributing the next student in the queue to the first available recruiter in a specific booth.
11. "Snooze" & "Pause" Queue Controls: Allowing recruiters to temporarily stop incoming students for breaks without losing the existing queue data.
13. Booth Capacity Monitor: Logic that prevents a queue from growing beyond a certain limit to manage student expectations.
14. Queue "Leaver" Protocol: Handling the logic when a student loses connection or manually leaves a queue, ensuring the next person is moved up immediately.
